# Asset credits

Codex built the gameplay, terrain, buildings, street furniture, generic pedestrians, officers, police cruisers and procedural vehicle details. The garage previews were rendered from the playable models.

The Jean Philanthrope and Mara Vega models and their walk and idle animations were generated for Neon Coast using Higgsfield, Tripo and rigging tools. Mara Vega is an original fictional character. Jean’s design draws on the requested AI character. The Strada GT, Mesa Utility, Porsche 911 Turbo S, Lamborghini Countach, Mercedes-AMG G 63 and Harbor Pickup exteriors were generated for the game. Vehicle names identify the depicted designs.

Poly Haven supplied the asphalt and plaster surface maps and the environment lighting under CC0. The interface requests Barlow Condensed and DM Sans through Google Fonts, with local fallback fonts. The game uses original and generated artwork inspired by open-city games and internet characters.
